# Mohammed — Remote Sensing & GIS

<img src="/img/Pic.jpeg" alt="Portfolio photo" align="right" width="200">

I am a Research Assistant in the Department of Geography and Planning at the University of Saskatchewan, where I completed my MSc in Geography under the supervision of Dr. Xulin Guo. My current work supports the Alberta Oil Sands Monitoring program, running a large-scale Landsat time-series pipeline over roughly 142,000 km² of the Athabasca Oil Sands Region from 1985 to the present, using LandTrendr and CCDC in Google Earth Engine to characterize vegetation disturbance and recovery.
